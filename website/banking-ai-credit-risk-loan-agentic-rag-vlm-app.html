AI Architecture: RAG + LLM + VLM + Agentic Banking Workflow

Reference architecture for governed banking decisions. The model assists and recommends; final credit approval remains policy-controlled with human review for high-risk cases.

Designed for CTO / Risk / Product review
1
Experience LayerRetail/HNI chatbot, relationship-manager console, loan officer workspace, risk dashboard and mobile banking entry points.
2
Secure API + Identity LayerAPI Gateway, OAuth/OIDC, consent capture, RBAC, customer segmentation, rate limits and audit headers.
3
Document + Image IntakePDFs, bank statements, salary slips, PAN/Aadhaar-like KYC, Excel portfolios, PPT proposals, screenshots, images and scanned forms.
4
Cloud Extraction APISigned upload URL or ingestion endpoint extracts text, tables, image objects, metadata and OCR/VLM descriptions into cloud storage.
5
Banking Source SystemsCore banking, CRM, LOS/LMS, KYC/CDD, bureau/CIBIL-like feeds, card/UPI transactions, AML/fraud monitoring and customer service logs.

Shared Agentic Flow

1
Classify intentLoan eligibility, credit risk, fraud alert, KYC gap, HNI portfolio, service request or document analysis.
2
Extract and normalizeOCR/VLM, table extraction, entity recognition, customer matching and document quality checks.
3
Retrieve evidenceRAG over policy, product rules, KYC checklist, risk policy, pricing grid and historical customer signals.
4
Run specialist agentsCredit risk, affordability, fraud, anomaly, KYC, loan product, HNI advisory and compliance agents.
5
Score and recommendRisk grade, eligible products, max ticket size, interest band, collateral need and manual review trigger.
6
Govern and learnHuman review, reason codes, drift checks, hallucination tests, prompt logs and feedback into evaluation sets.
6
RAG + Knowledge LayerPolicy chunks, embeddings, vector search, reranking, access filters, citations, product rules and customer-safe summaries.
7
LLM Reasoning LayerOpenAI or Claude produces grounded explanations, customer-facing responses, banker notes and reason-code narratives.
8
VLM LayerVision model reads screenshots, uploaded identity images, statement screenshots, collateral photos and scanned document layouts.
9
Risk + Decision ServicesDeterministic policy engine for DTI, bureau score, suspicious activity, anomaly detection, delinquency, affordability and exceptions.
10
Controls + ObservabilityPII masking, encryption, prompt-injection checks, consent, model evaluation, latency/cost monitoring, audit trail and reviewer queue.
